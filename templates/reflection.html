Weekly Reflection

Think out loud about your week — what moved, what stalled, what you want to change. Claude reads it alongside your current projects, goals, and tasks and proposes concrete changes you can review before anything is touched. Every reflection is kept forever for future reference.

    Speak as long as you want. Pause to think, then Resume to add more — each segment is added to your reflection text (visible on the Type tab). Click Done when finished to analyze.
